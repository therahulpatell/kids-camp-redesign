25+ Years of Trust

Since 2000, generations of families have chosen Kids Camp as their child's first home away from home. Our legacy is built on consistency, care, and countless success stories.

💖

Child-First Philosophy

Every decision we make starts with one question: "Is this best for the child?" From our curriculum to our meals, your little one's wellbeing is our north star.

🎨

Play-Based Learning

We believe children learn best when they're having fun. Our activities blend education with imagination, turning every moment into an opportunity for growth and discovery.

🛡

Safe & Nurturing Space

From child-proofed classrooms to trained staff and monitored environments, we create a cocoon of safety where children can explore freely and parents can breathe easily.

Our Founder

A Vision Built On Love,
Learning & Childhood

"When I started Kids Camp in 2000, I had one simple dream: to create a place where children could be children — curious, messy, loud, creative, and completely themselves."

Mrs. Namrata Anil Patel is a certified professional in Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE), Child Psychology, and Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP). With over 25 years of hands-on experience, she has transformed Kids Camp into a beloved institution where education meets empathy.

As a certified parenting coach and counselor, she doesn't just shape young minds — she strengthens entire families. Her vision continues to inspire a team of passionate educators who see every child as a unique universe of potential.

Mrs. Namrata Anil Patel
Founder & CEO, Kids Camp Play School
Learning Adventures

Programs That Feel
Like Adventures

Every program is designed to spark joy, build confidence, and prepare your child for a world full of possibilities.

🏰

Play Group

Ages 2 – 3 Years

Their first big adventure! A gentle introduction to structured play, social interaction, and the magic of learning through songs, stories, and sensory exploration.

🌸

Nursery

Ages 3 – 4 Years

Where little buds begin to bloom. Focus on language development, motor skills, creative expression, and building the confidence to ask "Why?"

📚

Junior KG

Ages 4 – 5 Years

The adventure deepens! Pre-reading, pre-math, and scientific curiosity come alive through hands-on projects, field trips, and collaborative play.

🎓

Senior KG

Ages 5 – 6 Years

Ready for the big leap! Advanced literacy, numeracy, and life skills that ensure a smooth, confident transition to primary school.

🌞

Day Care

Ages 2 – 10 Years

A home away from home for working parents. Safe, stimulating, and full of love — with nutritious meals, rest time, and engaging activities.

🎨

Activity Club

Ages 3 – 12 Years

After-school adventures in art, music, dance, sports, and more. Because learning doesn't stop when the bell rings!

A Day At Kids Camp

From Morning Giggles
To Goodnight Smiles

Every day is a new chapter in your child's adventure story. Here's how the magic unfolds.

8:00 AM

Morning Welcome

Warm hugs, bright smiles, and a gentle start to the day. Children are greeted individually, helping them transition from home to their second home.

🌞
9:00 AM

Circle Time

Songs, stories, and sharing circles that build language skills, emotional intelligence, and the confidence to speak up in a group.

🎤
10:00 AM

Creative Exploration

Art, craft, science experiments, and hands-on projects that turn curiosity into creation. Every child discovers their unique superpower.

🎨
11:00 AM

Snack Break

Nutritious, delicious, and shared with friends. We teach table manners, healthy eating, and the joy of community over a meal.

🍰
11:30 AM

Outdoor Play

Sunshine, swings, and silly races! Physical play builds strong bodies, sharp minds, and the social skills that last a lifetime.

🏃
12:30 PM

Storytelling

Books come alive with animated voices, puppet shows, and interactive tales. Imagination takes flight, one story at a time.

📖
1:00 PM

Goodbye Smiles

A day of adventures ends with proud show-and-tell, warm goodbyes, and excited stories to share with you on the way home.
